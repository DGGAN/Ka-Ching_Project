Privacy Policy — Ka-Ching!

Effective Date: November 2026 | Compliance: Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010)

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Your username, email address, and encrypted login password (or Google Sign-In ID).
  • Your Financial Records: Expenses, income, scanned receipt totals, and monthly budgets that you choose to save in the app.
  • Optional Smart Auto-Capture: This feature is OFF by default. Only when you turn it ON and grant phone Notification Access will Ka-Ching! read payment alerts containing RM amounts from these 10 currently supported Malaysian apps: Touch 'n Go eWallet, MAE by Maybank2u, Grab / GrabPay, CIMB OCTO MY, MyPB (Public Bank), RHB Mobile Banking, HLB Connect, Boost eWallet, Shopee / ShopeePay, and GXBank. Personal chats (WhatsApp, SMS, social media) are strictly ignored on your device.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to organize your spending, send budget alerts, and show personal spending summaries. We never sell or rent your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Your Control & Contact

You can turn off Smart Auto-Capture, mute alerts, unlink Google Sign-In, or edit and delete your records anytime in the Ka-Ching! app. For any privacy or data requests, email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
