Privacy Policy — Ka-Ching!

Effective Date: November 2026 | Compliance: Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010)

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Profile: Your username, email address, and encrypted authentication credentials (or Google OAuth identifier).
  • Financial Records: Transactions, receipts scanned via OCR, and budget limits that you voluntarily record or approve.
  • Opt-In Payment Alerts: Only when you explicitly enable Smart Auto-Capture and grant OS Notification Access, Ka-Ching! reads payment notifications from whitelisted Malaysian banking/eWallet apps containing RM amounts. Personal chats are never stored.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to categorize expenses, generate 50/30/20 budget alerts, and compute personal spending forecasts. We never sell or rent your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. User Control & Data Deletion

You may disable Smart Auto-Capture, revoke permissions, unbind Google OAuth, or edit/delete your records anytime inside the Ka-Ching! Profile screen.
